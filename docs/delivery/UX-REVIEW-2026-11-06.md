It changes no approved requirement: [PRD](../PRD.md) still owns behavior and [DESIGN](../DESIGN.md) owns the UI system.

Provenance: agent heuristic review on 2026-11-06 against the PRD users and setup, the DESIGN personas, principles and voice, the working-tree source, and the same day's emulator passes ([optional amounts](evidence/F07-OPTIONAL-AMOUNTS-2026-11-06.md), [remaining non-phone checks](evidence/EMULATOR-HARDENING-2026-11-06.md)). It is expert judgement, not user testing. TalkBack and the phone were not part of it, so the accessibility score is provisional.

## Who the app is for

- **PRD users:** students and employees in the Philippines tracking amounts they owe and their due dates; offline Android app, sideloaded APK, PHP only, Asia/Manila dates, no account.
- **DESIGN personas:** a busy student (assignments, exam dates: add task → date → reminder → complete) and a household organizer (rent, utilities, renewals: add bill/subscription → recurrence → reminders → calendar).
- **DESIGN principles:** (1) next actionable date first; (2) one primary action per screen; (3) status readable without colour; (4) errors recoverable without data loss; (5) decoration never obscures information.

## Rating

Overall about **6.5 / 10**: a solid, consistent foundation with a few weekly frictions for these users.

| Area | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Errors and recovery (principle 4) | 8.5 | Failed saves keep input, startup errors offer Retry, deletion names the due, repeated taps do not duplicate; the startup-Add gap was fixed on 2026-11-06. No Undo (excluded in v1). |
| Look and consistency | 8 | Focus system on every page; one ledger row and one status badge; badges pair a glyph with a word (principle 3 met); dark mode works; calm, specific copy matches the DESIGN voice. Large headings cost space, most visibly in landscape. |
| Navigation and structure | 7 | Five-slot bar with a centred Add is clear. Dues and Dashboard are two lists of the same dues and only Dashboard can search. Bar captions truncate at 200% text and on 320 dp screens. |
| Adding and editing | 7 | Essential-first order, numeric keyboard, preview, inline errors with focus, category-aware amount text; the keyboard no longer hides Save. Type sits below Amount although Type decides whether Amount is required; the date field reads `2026-11-06`; the number keyboard offers `,` and `-`, which are then rejected. |
| Fit to the Philippine setup | 7 | ₱ formatting, Manila dates, fully offline. Details shows Created/Updated in UTC. Reminders can arrive late under OEM battery savers common on phones sold locally; the app reports restrictions and links to settings. |
| Accessibility (provisional) | 6.5 | 200% text mostly holds, targets meet 44/48 dp, descriptions are rich. A full TalkBack pass is deferred; the Dashboard counters are tappable borders, not native buttons. |
| Core daily job | 6 | Recording and finding dues works. Marking a due paid, the most frequent action, takes about five taps and a scroll. Dues, the start page, lists every paid past due first in date order, so the next actionable due sinks over time (against principle 1). |
| Trust in saved data | 4 | No export or backup, and Android backup is deliberately off; losing or replacing the phone loses every due. A near-loss happened on 2026-11-06 when a deploy from a second computer uninstalled the app (data kept only because of `-k`). |

By persona: the busy student is served well; the household organizer is served poorly, because without recurrence rent and utilities must be re-entered every month.
