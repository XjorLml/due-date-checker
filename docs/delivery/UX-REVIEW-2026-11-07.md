# UI/UX review — 2026-11-07 (second audit)

Requested by the user on 2026-11-07 ("audit the ui/ux again"), after all sixteen decisions from the [first review](UX-REVIEW-2026-11-06.md) shipped in five waves. It uses the same users, principles, areas and impact method, so the two ratings compare. It records findings only; it changes no approved requirement ([PRD](../PRD.md) owns behavior, [DESIGN](../DESIGN.md) the UI system), and none of the new findings is decided yet. Options, recommendations and the proposed order are in [UX improvements, round 2](../../specs/ux-improvements-2/spec.md) and its [plan](../../specs/ux-improvements-2/plan.md).

Provenance: agent heuristic review on 2026-11-07 against the PRD users and setup, the DESIGN personas, principles and voice, and the working-tree source. Screens observed on the Android 35 emulator, with seeded sample dues:

- Dues, Dashboard, Calendar, Settings, Reminders, Details and the editor.
- Light and dark mode; 100% and 200% text; 411 dp and 320 dp widths; landscape.
- The Wave 1–5 evidence of the previous two days.

It is expert judgement, not user testing. TalkBack and the phone were again not part of it, so the accessibility score stays provisional.

## Who the app is for

Unchanged from the [first review](UX-REVIEW-2026-11-06.md#who-the-app-is-for):

- **PRD users:** students and employees in the Philippines tracking what they owe and when; offline Android, PHP, Asia/Manila.
- **DESIGN personas:** the busy student and the household organizer.
- **DESIGN principles:** (1) next actionable date first; (2) one primary action per screen; (3) status readable without colour; (4) errors recoverable without data loss; (5) decoration never obscures information.

## Rating

Overall about **7.8 / 10**, up from about 6.5. The weekly frictions the first review found are gone. What remains is mostly about reminders, which are the app's main protection against a missed payment, and about how much of each screen goes to explanation rather than dues.

| Area | Before | Now | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Errors and recovery (principle 4) | 8.5 | 9 | Confirmation after Save, payment and Delete; 5-second Undo that reverses only that deletion; import is all-or-nothing; the migration runner no longer hides a failing step. The editor still drops typed input on Back without asking (UX22). |
| Look and consistency | 8 | 8.5 | Themed messages in both modes, quiet "No amount", compact landscape headings, counters that look the same and act as buttons. Developer-style captions repeat on most pages (UX26), and Calendar counts are bare digits (UX24). |
| Navigation and structure | 7 | 7.5 | Bar captions stay whole at 200% and 320 dp. Dues and Dashboard still overlap and only Dashboard searches (UX09 kept as decided). Delete is only inside the editor (UX27). |
| Adding and editing | 7 | 8 | Type before Amount, `06 Nov 2026`, decimal keyboard, Repeat for bills. The amount field carries three helper lines plus a preview (UX26), and there is no unsaved-changes prompt (UX22). |
| Fit to the Philippine setup | 7 | 8 | Manila timestamps on Details; Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO steps (wording unverified on the phone). Notifications stay generic for privacy, which is right for a shared phone but tells the user nothing until they open the app. |
| Accessibility (provisional) | 6.5 | 7 | Counters are buttons; captions and the calendar fit narrow screens; messages announce themselves through Android. Still no TalkBack pass, so announcements and the Undo timeout are unobserved. |
| Core daily job | 6 | 8 | Mark as paid is one tap from Details, earlier paid dues fold away, and monthly bills repeat. Reminders are off until set up and nothing on the main pages says so (UX17), and the Dashboard's peso figure leaves out overdue bills (UX18). |
| Trust in saved data | 4 | 6.5 | Export and import, Undo, and the hardened migrations. Export is manual with no last-export date or nudge (UX21); the file is plain text by design; the user's phone still runs an older build signed elsewhere, so none of this protects their data yet. |

By persona:

- **Busy student:** served well. Tasks without amounts, Calendar, quick payment toggle.
- **Household organizer:** now served (recurring rent and utilities, export), but most likely to be hurt by reminders that were never switched on and by a Dashboard total that hides overdue bills.

## New findings

Impact uses reach × frequency × severity, each 1–3, as in the [plan](../../specs/ux-improvements/plan.md#ranking-method). IDs continue from UX16 and stay fixed. "Rule affected" names the approved text a change would touch.

| ID | Finding | Impact | Rule affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| UX17 | Reminders are off on every installation, and nothing on Dues, Dashboard or Details says so. The Dues page has a plain "Reminders" chip with no state; only Settings shows "Off". A user who never opens Reminders gets no warning before a due date. | 12 | PRD F05 ("Reminders start off… no permission request on launch") stays; a visible state or one-time suggestion needs DESIGN, and PRD review if it prompts |
| UX18 | The Dashboard's large peso figure covers known unpaid amounts from today to month end only. With ₱12,850 overdue it shows "₱1,699.00", which reads as what is owed. The caption explains it in small text. | 12 | PRD F02 upcoming-total rule |
| UX19 | The Reminders page is a long form: status card → Schedule → time → Quiet hours → two time fields → seven stacked full-width buttons. Two buttons are styled primary ("Save schedule", "Save and enable reminders"), against principle 2. Turning reminders on is the last thing on the page, and "Disable reminders", "Test notification" and "Refresh / Retry" show even while reminders are off. Status lines read technically ("Battery optimization is active…", "Newly elapsed times are skipped"). | 9 | DESIGN F05 reminder adaptation; principle 2 |
| UX20 | The start page shows few dues per screen. Brand lockup, heading, caption and Reminders chip take about 28% of the screen at 100% text and about half at 200% (2½ rows visible). Each row is about 88 dp tall because the badge sits under the amount. | 9 | DESIGN "Dues list (as built)", ledger row |
| UX21 | Export is manual and silent: no "last exported" date and no nudge after many changes. A user who never exports is still one lost phone from losing every due. | 9 | None for a date line; PRD UX05 if the app reminds the user |
| UX22 | Back or Cancel in the editor discards a typed due without asking. | 6 | PRD "An unsaved-changes prompt is not part of approved v1 behavior" |
| UX23 | An overdue due says "Overdue" but not how late (list, Details, Dashboard). | 4 | DESIGN status badge / F02 status text |
| UX24 | Calendar day counts are bare digits under the day number ("3 / 2", "7 / 1"), which read like part of the date; the explanation is a caption above the grid. | 4 | DESIGN calendar day cell |
| UX25 | Settings choices (Appearance, Start of week) and the Reminders day-before switch change nothing until a Save button further down the page; tapping "Dark" gives no visible result. | 4 | PRD F04 and F05 draft-until-Save rules |
| UX26 | Captions that read like developer notes repeat across pages and push content down. Examples: "Date order · all active saved dues · amounts in PHP", "Saved information · Amounts in PHP · Dates use Asia/Manila", "Numbers count all saved dues, including Paid". The amount field has three helper lines plus a preview, and Details repeats "Back to list" under the app bar's back arrow. | 3 | DESIGN voice and page anatomy |
| UX27 | Delete is reachable only through Details → Edit due → the "Delete" text in the editor's app bar. | 2 | V1.3 soft-delete spec (editor-only Delete) |

Strengths to keep: recovery behaviour and Undo; status without colour; calm, specific confirmation wording; one-tap payment; repeating bills; offline operation with a user-owned export; generic notifications.

## Open verification (not design findings)

- **Phone:** nothing from Waves 1–5 has run on the user's phone. Its app is signed by another computer, so it must be reinstalled from that laptop first.
- **TalkBack:** never exercised. That covers message announcements, the Undo time extension, counter roles and focus order.
- **OEM pieces:** the phone's keyboard (minus key) and the Xiaomi steps' wording.
- **Notifications:** no reminder has been delivered on the current build. The last natural delivery was observed on the phone on 2026-11-04 ([retest](evidence/F05-RETEST-2026-11-04.md)), before Waves 1–5.
