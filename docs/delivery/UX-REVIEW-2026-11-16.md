## Who the app is for

Unchanged: students and employees in the Philippines tracking what they owe and when, offline, PHP, Asia/Manila. The household organizer (rent, utilities, subscriptions, often paid per payroll cutoff) gets the most value and is the user this review weighs most.

## Rating

Overall about **8 / 10**, up from about 7.8. Unlocked reminders now name the due and can mark it paid, and bills repeat monthly or twice a month. What limits the value now is mostly **the reminder itself**: it is easy to miss, sounds like every other app, stops once a due is overdue and cannot be postponed. Next comes **money by payday**: totals run to month end rather than to the next cutoff, and bills whose amount changes each month carry last month's amount forward.

## Ringtone and alert recommendations (UX29–UX31)

Android facts that shape the options:

- **Sound belongs to the channel.** Since Android 8 a notification's sound, vibration and banner style belong to its channel. After the channel is created only the user can change them, in system settings. The app can choose sensible defaults only when it creates a channel, so a different default means a new channel ID.
- **Full-screen and exact alarms are restricted.** Full-screen, alarm-style alerts are reserved for alarm and calling apps (a special permission on Android 14+). Exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) also need the user's grant. Both are poor fits for bill reminders.

Recommended, in order:

1. **Fix the wrong privacy text (UX28).** One sentence: "Unlocked, a reminder shows the due's title, amount and date. The lock screen only says you have a due. Notes are never shown."
2. **Add a visible "Sound & alert style" row (UX29).** Put it on the Reminders page, outside the collapsed section, showing the current state ("Default sound · no banner"). It opens Android's page for that channel, where the user picks any ringtone or vibration. Let "Test notification" work before reminders are enabled, as a preview.
3. **Split reminders into two channels (UX29):**
   - **Due today:** High importance, so it shows a banner and makes a sound.
   - **Coming up:** the day before, Default importance.

   Users can then give "due today" a distinct ringtone. Optionally ship one short, recognisable Duewise chime as the first default, which the user can still change. Old channel settings cannot be carried over, so the change needs a one-time note: "We split reminders into Due today and Coming up; check their sounds."
4. **Add one overdue nudge (UX30).** Remind at the reminder time on the day after the due date ("Water bill is 1 day late"), then weekly until paid or deleted, inside quiet hours rules. It could go in the "Due today" channel or a third "Overdue" channel.
5. **Add "Remind me later" (UX31).** A second action next to Mark as paid, with fixed choices such as "In 3 hours" and "Tonight 7 PM", respecting quiet hours. Keep it to these two to avoid a settings maze.

Not recommended: an in-app ringtone picker (it would recreate channels behind the user's back), full-screen alarm-style alerts, and exact alarms. Delivery that is a few minutes late is acceptable for bills, and the page already says Android may deliver later.

## Other recommendations by value

- **UX32 — "Amount changes each month" switch on a repeating Bill.** When on, the next occurrence is created with "Amount needed" (counted separately on Dashboard, like Task "No amount"), and the reminder says "Check this month's amount". This needs a PRD change to let such a Bill hold no amount until the bill arrives.
- **UX33 — Payday setting (default 15 and 30) and a "This pay" figure.** Show on Dashboard "Before your next payday (30 Nov): ₱X", including overdue (which also settles UX18). It can share the days picker built for Twice a month.
- **UX20/UX26 — Give Dues back its rows.** Collapse search and filters behind one "Search & filter" row (expanding when a filter is active) and drop the caption. Aim for four or more rows at 100% text.
- **UX17 — Ask once on first launch.** Ask "Want a reminder before each due? You can change the time and sound later," then show reminder state on the bell (filled or crossed out).
- **UX34 — Status dot on calendar days.** Red for an overdue due that day, filled for unpaid and hollow for all paid, with the count kept for screen readers.
- **UX35 — Optional "Paid on" date and "Reference no."** when marking paid. Skip-able, never required.

## Strengths to keep

- Mark as paid from the notification.
- Calm and specific wording.
- Status shown without relying on colour.
- Undo and recoverable import.
- Twice-a-month repeats.
- Everything works offline with no account.
- The lock screen stays generic.
