New coverage:

- **Restore guard:** restore keeps every field and changes only the deletion and update time. It does nothing twice, for a different deletion stamp or for an unknown id. An edit still cannot revive a tombstone.
- **Editor messages:** "Due saved.", "Changes saved." and "Saved. Next one added for …"; nothing on failure. Delete records the exact stamp; nothing on cancel or failure.
- **Undo:** restores the row and plans reminders through the production reminder-aware repository, and raises the refresh event. Only the latest offer is honoured, once. A changed stamp gives "Nothing to undo."; a storage failure keeps the tombstone and says so.
- **Details:** a page showing a deleted due reloads it after Undo; timestamps are in Manila across UTC midnight (16:30 UTC → "06 Nov 2026, 00:30 Manila").

## Limits

- No phone check. The keyboard (Gboard or the Xiaomi keyboard may hide the minus key or may not) and the snackbar's position over the phone's navigation bar are unverified.
- No TalkBack (not on this emulator image): that each message is read once, and that a longer accessibility timeout lengthens the Undo time, rest on Material's documented behavior and are unobserved.
- Undo was exercised from Details only. Deleting always returns to Details, so Undo after navigating to a list within 5 seconds relies on the existing page refresh and is untested on the device.
- The decimal key listener follows the phone's decimal separator. A phone set to a comma-decimal language would accept "," and not "."; the Philippine English and Filipino settings use ".".
- Screenshots `w4-dashboard-counters`, `w4-keyboard`, `w4-toast-saved`, `w4-toast-paid`, `w4-toast-deleted`, `w4-toast-restored` and `w4-toast-dark-200` are in the session scratchpad, not committed.
