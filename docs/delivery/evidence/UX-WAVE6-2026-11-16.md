## Emulator checks

The emulator held the twice-a-month build: schema 7, 0 dues, reminders off, and the old channel `duewise_reminders_v1` present.

- **Upgrade (OD05, AV05):** installing over it kept the data. After launch `user_version` was 8 and `reminder_settings` read `1|0|1|540|1320|480|1`, so overdue defaults on.
- **Channels (AL01, AL02):** `dumpsys notification` showed `duewise_due_today_v1` at importance 4 (High) and `duewise_coming_up_v1` at 3, with `duewise_reminders_v1` marked `mDeleted=true`. The Reminders page showed the one-time message.
- **Sound & alert style (AL03):** the card appears outside the collapsed section. The rows read "Due today · On the due date and when overdue · Banner · Default (Pixie Dust)" and "Coming up · The day before · Sound, no banner · Default (Pixie Dust)". Changing a sound in Android's page and returning was not exercised.
- **Test reminder (AL04):** with reminders off, "Play a test reminder" posted `duewise-test` on `duewise_due_today_v1` at importance 4, and `reminder_settings.enabled` stayed 0.
- **Overdue and "Amount needed" (OD01, OD02, AV04):** tagged temporary dues were seeded through `run-as sqlite3`, and reminders were turned on with the time set three minutes ahead. The water bill (due 15 Nov) was queued for a 1-day-late reminder at 15:46 Manila, alongside Meralco (amount varies, no amount, due 16 Nov). Both posted at importance 4: "UXQA Water bill is 1 day late · ₱850.00 · Sun 15 Nov · repeats monthly" with Mark as paid, and "UXQA Meralco is due today · Amount needed · Mon 16 Nov · repeats monthly".
- **Tap (OD04):** the tap meant for Mark as paid hit the notification body. Android sent its tap intent and cancelled it, and the payment was not exercised; the App test covers it. Replaying the tap intent (`OPEN_REMINDER` with the overdue key) opened the water bill's Details.
- **Dashboard (PD03):** "Before payday · 30 Nov", ₱7,100.00 (water ₱850 overdue + rent ₱6,250; SSS on 30 Nov left to that payday), "3 unpaid · ₱850.00 overdue · 1 without an amount, excluded". The Meralco row showed "Amount needed".
- **Settings (PD01):** the Paydays card showed First payday 15 and Second payday 30. Saving was not exercised, to leave the emulator's preferences unchanged.
- **Editor (AV01):** on a new Bill with Repeat Monthly, the "Amount changes each month" row appeared. Ticking it changed Amount to "optional", with "Optional: fill it in when the bill arrives." and "Will save as “Amount needed” until you add it.". The draft was discarded.
- **Cleanup:** the app was force-stopped. Only the tagged rows and their reminder records were removed, leaving 0 dues and 0 occurrences. Reminder settings were restored to off at 09:00 (overdue stays at its new default, on), no Duewise notification remained, and the emulator was shut down.

Not checked: dark mode and 200% text for the new card and rows; TalkBack; a changed ringtone being read back; Mark as paid on a live overdue notification; a day-before reminder on Coming up.
