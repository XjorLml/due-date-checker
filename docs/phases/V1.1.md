## Delivered behavior

- Add a due with a required title, PHP amount, and calendar date, plus optional notes.
- Trim titles, reject blank titles and invalid/out-of-range amounts, round to centavos, and accept zero amounts and past dates.
- Default the date to today in Asia/Manila using an injected TimeProvider. Store mutation timestamps as UTC.
- Preview the rounded peso amount; normalize blank notes to null.
- List active dues by due date, then creation timestamp, with title, formatted amount/date, and paid/unpaid text. Paid rows remain visible; the paid control is part of V1.2.
- Show loading, empty, validation, and retryable storage-error states. Keep the form after save failure and retain previously loaded rows after refresh failure.
- Reload on returning to the list and on app resume. Prevent overlapping saves and duplicate inserts when returning to the list fails after a successful save.
