Four items were decided on 2026-11-07, when the user asked to align the app with the live prototype. For round 2 they chose "the prototype settles those three" (UX19, UX22, UX25), and for the calendar "use '2 dues'" (UX24). These four are built under the [prototype alignment spec](../prototype-alignment/spec.md), not this plan. The other seven are chosen in the [decision lab](#html-decision-lab), as in round 1.

## HTML decision lab

Round 2 was added on 2026-11-07 to the same **UX decisions** tab of the [design exploration](../../prototypes/design-exploration/index.html#ux-decisions), at the user's "proceed". The tab now holds two rounds: round 2 (open, the default) and round 1 (decided, kept as a record). Like round 1, it is a non-normative decision aid, and choosing there records a browser-local preference, not a decision. Round 1 keeps its scenarios [DL01–DL08](../ux-improvements/spec.md#html-decision-lab). DL09–DL13 add:
